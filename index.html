Comprehensive Learning: Learning takes place from simple to complex, known to unknown. At 4 Supports Therapy, we understand you as a whole person. Our work is grounded in the Biopsychosocial Model NCBI Source — Biological (your body, brain, health), Psychological (your thoughts, emotions, trauma, meaning), Social (your family, culture, faith, community). No part heals alone. [See Source]

● Biological — Body & Brain ● Psychological — Thoughts & Trauma ● Social — Family & Culture

True healing connects all three. This is why we offer 4 Supports: Psychological, Social, Educational (Psychopedagogy) & Spiritual.
