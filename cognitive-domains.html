Comprehensive Learning: Learning takes place from simple to complex, known to unknown.

Comprehensive Learning: Learning takes place from simple to complex, known to unknown. An education psychologist, by the name Benjamin Bloom (1956) founded cognitive domains of learning stipulated under 6 hierarchical levels [See Bloom's Original Work].

1. Remembering / Knowledge

Retrieving, recalling, recognizing facts

📚 Primary:
Recite multiplication table (2x2=4), name parts of plant
🎓 High School:
List causes of WW1, define photosynthesis
🎓 Tertiary:
Recall DSM-5-TR criteria for depression (Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition, Text Revision), list counselling theories
🌱 Adaptive Learning:
Remember steps of handwashing (water, soap, scrub, rinse), remember own name & home, recall morning routine

2. Understanding / Comprehension

Explaining meaning in own words

📚 Primary:
Explain why plants need water, summarize story
🎓 High School:
Explain mitosis vs meiosis, interpret graph
🎓 Tertiary:
Explain attachment theory to client, summarize research article
🌱 Adaptive Learning:
Understand WHY we wash hands (remove germs), why we say please/thank you

3. Applying

Using knowledge in real situation

📚 Primary:
Use addition to buy snacks, apply grammar to write sentence
🎓 High School:
Apply quadratic formula, apply lab safety rules
🎓 Tertiary:
Apply CBT technique to case study, apply ethical code
🌱 Adaptive Learning:
Apply handwashing before eating, use money to buy bread

4. Analyzing

Breaking down, finding connections

📚 Primary:
Compare domestic vs wild animals, sort shapes
🎓 High School:
Analyze poem for themes, compare political systems
🎓 Tertiary:
Analyze client transcript for distortions, break research into variables
🌱 Adaptive Learning:
Break making tea into steps, sort dirty vs clean clothes

5. Evaluating

Justifying, critiquing, judging

📚 Primary:
Judge fair way to share toys, evaluate story ending
🎓 High School:
Evaluate best energy for Kenya, debate position
🎓 Tertiary:
Evaluate therapy intervention, critique methodology
🌱 Adaptive Learning:
Evaluate if hands clean enough, decide if shoes correct

6. Creating - Highest Level!

Producing new original work

📚 Primary:
Create own story with drawings, invent new game
🎓 High School:
Design science project, write poem, create business plan
🎓 Tertiary:
Create intervention program, develop research, create therapy model
🌱 Adaptive Learning:
Create own visual routine chart, create sandwich, organize room - INDEPENDENCE!

All learners irrespective of learning abilities can reach the highest level of creating. They only need conducive environment and inclusive strategies.

Why This Matters at 4 Supports Therapy

We use Bloom's Taxonomy to ensure no learner is stuck at remembering only. True functioning = Creating. This is comprehensive learning. From simple to complex, known to unknown.
