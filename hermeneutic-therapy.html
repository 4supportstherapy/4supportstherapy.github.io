Hermeneutic Therapy Model

Where Sacred Text Meets Human Story - An Original Model by Hannah Wanjuhi Ticha

Every person lives by an interpretation. How you interpret your trauma determines if you remain a victim or become a victor. How you interpret scripture determines if you find freedom or shame. Hermeneutic Therapy helps you interpret both correctly.

What is Hermeneutics?

Hermeneutics is the art and science of interpretation. Originally used for interpreting sacred texts, I have developed it into a clinical model for interpreting human pain. We don't just listen to your story — we help you re-interpret it with truth, grace, and clinical wisdom.

Why Hermeneutics Matters Today

The Bible, being the most read book in the whole world, does in no doubt attract a myriad of interpretations. Different people would interpret particular parts of the scripture differently. Politicians, and other leaders would pick verses from the bible in isolation and interpret them in accordance to their needs at the time. Religious leaders too have different interpretations. At times these interpretations lead to sharp divisions and at times causes psychological turmoil to the believers threatening the very peace that the bible teaching is supposed to spearhead. Different interpretations include gender religious roles, days of worship, dressing, marriage, gender roles in families among others. These differences at times could lead to challenges that threaten the faith of the affected. It is against this background that biblical hermeneutics need to be understood today.

Biblical Hermeneutics is the science of interpretation of the bible and the methods of interpreting it. From the Greek root meaning interpret or interpretation. Merriam Webster defines Hermeneutics as the study of the methodological principles of interpretation as of the bible. This means that it could also be interpretation of another book, song or poem. At 4 Supports Therapy, our counsellors endeavor to explain the relationship among, Hermeneutics, exegesis and eisegesis in order to explain the objective interpretations of the scripture and objectively relate it with the experiences at hand. Good Hermeneutics reduce doctrinal errors. Thus integrating biblical hermeneutics with counselling psychology.

The Three Layers of My Hermeneutic Model:

1. Textual Hermeneutics: For clients who want it, we explore what scripture truly says about your situation — not what culture or hurt told you it says. Healing from misinterpreted scripture that caused shame.

2. Narrative Hermeneutics: Your life is a story. Trauma wrote a chapter, but not the ending. We reinterpret your story from "damaged" to "becoming" using clinical tools and spiritual truth.

3. Clinical Hermeneutics: Symptoms have meaning. Anxiety is interpreting future as dangerous. Depression is interpreting self as worthless. We uncover and correct the faulty interpretation at the root.
