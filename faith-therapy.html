Faith-Integrated Counselling & Spirituality

Faith and mental health are not separate. At 4 Supports Therapy, Faith-Integrated Counselling respects your beliefs. As a Christian therapist, I integrate clinical expertise with Biblical wisdom — but only when you invite me to.

Spirituality is more than religion. It is your connection to purpose, values, forgiveness, and grace. Whether you are a strong believer, questioning, or healing from church hurt, your spiritual journey is welcome here.

1. Clinically Grounded, Spiritually Sensitive
Evidence-based therapy + Faith as resource.

2. Client-Led, Never Imposed
You choose how much faith you want in sessions.

3. Healing Whole Person
Mind, body, soul, and spirit.
