Faith-Integrated Counselling & Spirituality

Clinically Grounded. Spiritually Sensitive. Client-Led.

Faith and mental health are not separate. For many people, spirituality is the foundation of healing. Faith gives meaning to pain, hope in trauma, and strength in brokenness.

At 4 Supports Therapy, Faith-Integrated Counselling respects your beliefs. As a Christian therapist, I integrate clinical expertise with Biblical wisdom — but only when you invite me to. Your faith leads, I follow.

Spirituality is more than religion. It is your connection to purpose, values, forgiveness, and grace. Whether you are a strong believer, questioning, or healing from church hurt, your spiritual journey is welcome here.

What Faith-Integration Looks Like:

1. Clinically Grounded, Spiritually Sensitive
We use evidence-based therapy: Bio-Psycho-Social + CAFT + Trauma-Informed Care. Faith is an added resource, not a replacement for science.

2. Client-Led, Never Imposed
You choose how much faith you want in sessions. Some clients want prayer, scripture, and Christian tools. Others just want a therapist who understands their values. Both are honored.

3. Healing the Whole Person
We address mind, body, soul, and spirit. True healing touches anxiety, relationships, past trauma, AND spiritual wounds.

This Service Is For:

• Christians seeking counselling that honors their faith
• Couples wanting God at the center of their marriage work (CAFT + Faith)
• Anyone exploring purpose, forgiveness, grief, and meaning
• Those hurt by religion who need safe, non-judgmental spiritual care

My approach is ethical, inclusive, and respectful. I will never preach at you. I will walk with you — clinically competent, spiritually compassionate.

CAFT Model + Faith = Marriage built on science AND sacred covenant.
